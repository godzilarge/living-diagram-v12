# Independent review: brick 3 of the v2 migration, the panel (2026-10-16)

**0 critical, 2 high, 11 medium, 8 low.**

Reviewer: `relecteur-ui`, without the author's conclusions. Report recorded as rendered; the treatment is at the end
of the document. Yardstick: `design/comps/shell/index.html` at rest and in its `panel-wide` / `panel-focus` states
(measured in Chromium at 1440 × 900, light and dark), `DIRECTION.md` § "The panel, three widths", `DESIGN.md`
(Facts, Alerts, Panel rows), `PRODUCT.md` § Capabilities, `docs/13-migration-plan.md` row 3, `.claude/rules/app.md`.

**Suites**: `npm run check` green (155 tests). `npm run e2e` green (46 tests, 5 of `panel.spec.ts`).

**What passed the probes**: compact rows are 32 px with a 28 px head; the panel sits at 984,76 and is 440 × 748;
dark-theme contrast holds (ink-3 6.27:1, error 4.98:1); reduced motion is covered by `base.css`; no check code is
visible. Probes in `2026-10-16-panel-sondes/` (`zz-revue-sonde.spec.ts`, `zz-revue-sonde2.spec.ts`, captures); the
work tree was left as found.

## High

- **H1** `app/src/state/panel.ts:102-125`: `PRODUCT.md` and plan row 3 require every interface fact of the contract.
  Not shown at any width: `type`, `parent_interface`, `virtual_context`, `roles`, and `description_parsed`
  (criticality, options). The catalogue keys `panel:interfaces.type.*` exist but nothing reads them. Probe: compare
  `InterfaceRow` with `SnapshotInterface` (`engine/src/contracts/snapshot.ts:300-391`).
- **H2** `app/src/panel/StructureSections.tsx:106,146`: an HA member that is "up" and a stack member that is "ready"
  are painted green. DESIGN.md (State › Ok) says green means a port that is up or a bundled member, "Nowhere else".
  Probe `green-words`: `{"text":"up","table":"DC01-EDGE","color":"rgb(20, 120, 60)"}` ×2 on dc01-fw-01.

## Medium

- **M1** `app/src/state/panel.ts:131-138`: the filter never matches the words the table shows. The plan promises
  "every word typed". Probe (All scope, dc01-core-01): `Up`, `Down`, `trunk`, `Access`, `never` and `full` each give
  0 rows.
- **M2** `app/src/panel/DevicePanel.tsx:85-91`: "Changes since" shows raw contract values ("Collecte success →
  partial" in the French UI). Its fallback prints the raw field path and `JSON.stringify` of objects (`stack`,
  `evidence`). PRODUCT asks for plain words. Probe `changes-fr`: dc01-core-02, from=demo-0112.
- **M3** `app/src/panel/DevicePanel.tsx:107`: "Seen by" shows `w.source.toUpperCase()`, so the stub reads "CDP,
  DESCRIPTION, LLDP". These are uppercase words (DESIGN Don'ts) that come from no catalogue. Capture
  `.impeccable/planche-panneau/panel-stub.png`.
- **M4** `app/src/panel/panel.css:137-139` (with `Table.css` wrap): focus rows reach 63 px when the mode cell and the
  vendor reason wrap. DESIGN "Panel rows" says 44 px rows, a value plus one second line, and the mockup keeps a
  steady 44 px. Probe `focus-rows`: `[63,63,63,45,45,45,45,45]`.
- **M5** `app/src/panel/panel.css:159`: in English the "Speed and duplex" header button is 114 px wide in a 104 px
  column. Its sort icon ends at x 379, 10 px past the column edge (369), and sits under the Media head, so the column
  shows no sort affordance. Probes `focus-headers` and `zz-sonde-focus-head.png`.
- **M6** `app/src/panel/DevicePanel.tsx:149`: in focus the "Interfaces 61 ports" heading stays visible. The mockup
  hides it (`index.html:431`, `.s-ports h3 { display: none }`), so the scope bar should sit right under the strip.
  Probe `focus-interfaces-heading: true`.
- **M7** `app/src/panel/cells.tsx:62` with `app/src/ui/Table.css:32`: the focus ring of a neighbour link is cut on its
  left side. The `td` has `overflow: hidden` and the link starts at the cell edge. DESIGN asks for a 2 px ring with a
  2 px offset. Probes `neighbour-link-focus` (`clippedLeft: true`) and `zz-sonde-link-focus.png`.
- **M8** `app/src/panel/PanelFrame.tsx:43` and `app/src/diagram/Diagram.tsx:141`: closing the panel with the keyboard
  (Enter on ×) sends focus to `<body>`, so a keyboard user loses their place. Probe `focus-after-close:
  {"tag":"BODY"}`.
- **M9** `app/src/panel/panel.css:66,197`: the weight of a pressed rail button is defined a third time, after
  `shell/PrefsControls.css:46`, instead of once in the primitive. The ports bar (scope + filter at 148 px) is also
  copied from `primitives/page.css:129-173`. The two copies disagree: the primitives page (`.page-scope`) keeps the
  pressed scope at 500, the panel sets 600. app.md says a state has one definition.
- **M10** `app/src/panel/words.ts:111-128`: nothing tests the rule "never a code". `words.ts` has no unit tests at
  all: the fallback to the server's meaning, "N other checks", the speed, duration and last-change formats, and the
  detail line. `CODES` is a `Set<string>` that nothing ties to `CheckCode` or to the catalogue, and the cast on line
  125 hides a missing key. Only the e2e regex runs, and only on the fixture's codes.
- **M11** `app/src/panel/OtherPanels.tsx:54-59`: the cable panel does not show the cable's status (observed,
  documented, confirmed), its kind, its sources (`evidence`), or the checks on it. `PRODUCT.md` names "status of each
  cable … with its sources and its checks". Neither the cable nor the aggregate panel has a "To check" section.
  Capture `panel-cable-light-cobalt-en.png`.

## Low

- **L2** `app/src/panel/panel.css:63`: `gap: 6px` is off the 8 px grid and not a token. The 148 px filter and the

- **L3** `app/src/styles/tokens.css:179`: `--panel-row-height` is declared but never used.
- **L4** `app/src/ui/Panel.tsx:26` via `PanelFrame.tsx:47`: the panel's `aside` is named by the hostname alone. The

  this. Probe `aside-name: "dc01-core-01"`.
- **L5** `en/fr/panel.json:37,403`: unused keys `toCheck.nothing` and `cluster.members`. The aggregate members count
  borrows `identity.stackMembers` instead (`OtherPanels.tsx:104`).
- **L6** `app/src/panel/cells.tsx:61` and `app/src/state/panel.ts:316`: the separators `", "` and `" – "` are

- **L7** `app/src/panel/DevicePanel.tsx:57`: device capabilities are printed as raw tokens ("trans_bridge,

- **L8** `app/src/panel/DevicePanel.tsx:85`: `KNOWN` hinges on whether a translation is truthy, which is always the

# canon — the standing exit

**World**: none. This is the category standard played straight, executed at the level of the best products of its
kind (Linear, Figma, draw.io at their cleanest): the arrangement a user expects, at full craft. No signature move,
no world, no irony, no smuggled quirk.

**Thesis**: what this category ships, executed impeccably.

**Palette**
- Light neutral: white content surface, a second slightly cooler neutral for bars and panel, hairline borders.
- One accent, a working blue (not violet, not cyan): primary actions, selection, current state, focus.
- Ink near-black with two secondary steps tinted from it.
- Status colours (confirmed, observed, documented, error, warning, changed) carried by the standard semantic set,
  calibrated for contrast.

**Type**: a workhorse UI sans, system-grade (an OFL face with tabular figures: e.g. Public Sans, Source Sans 3,
Figtree; **not** Inter, which is the AI default, and not IBM Plex). One family, scale ratio 1.125–1.2, sentence case.

**Density**: the category's: comfortable, 8 px grid, 32 px controls, 13 px body, panels that scroll.

**Composition**: top bar (identity, infrastructure, run, view switch, layer choice, search, diff summary, export,
user), canvas, right panel with sections and a sortable interface table, run band at the bottom, canvas controls
bottom-left. Devices as clean rounded cards with a type icon and the name; cables as lines with status styling;
clusters as light frames.

**Scene**: light. Say how the dark theme would go (the standard inversion).

**Honest risk**: indistinguishable from the competition by design. Its only argument is execution: every alignment,
every spacing, every state, flawless.
